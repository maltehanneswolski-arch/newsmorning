COP31 collapses after the EU refuses a text without fossil fuels

COP31 in Antalya ended on Sunday without a decision, the first collapse since Copenhagen in 2009, after two weeks and two days of overtime made it the longest round of UN climate talks yet. A bloc around Saudi Arabia and Russia refused any mention of fossil fuels in the final text, and the EU-led coalition would not endorse one that left them out; the fracture ran between the Arab group and the Europeans. The same week, EU negotiators agreed to postpone parts of the bloc's own methane rules, and Australia, the co-host, blamed difficult geopolitical times.
