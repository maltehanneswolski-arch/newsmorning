Russian cruise missile lands in Poland during allied drills

A Russian cruise missile was among three objects that crossed into Polish airspace early on Friday, during one of Russia's heaviest air attacks on Ukraine: almost 100 missiles and around 400 drones, three Kyiv bridges over the Dnipro hit and at least eight dead in Kyiv and Lviv, days after Ukraine struck the Kerch bridge for a third time. Nobody was hurt in Poland, but Donald Tusk said it did not look like an accident because it coincided with Koalicja 2026, the UK- and France-led exercise for a postwar force in Ukraine; NATO's Mark Rutte called it a "grave violation" and Warsaw asked allies for more air defence. Estonia closed its border with Russia the same day, after eight Latvians were caught allegedly trying to burn down an Estonian drone maker.

Tuition fees hold up Burnham's Brexit reset

London and Brussels signed three deals at Friday's summit: UK alignment with EU food rules, with carve-outs on lab-grown meat, animal welfare and pesticides; a link between the two carbon markets that shields British exporters from EU carbon tariffs; and a youth mobility scheme for up to 100,000 young people a side for three years. EU sources say the deals will not enter into force until Britain cuts tuition fees for EU students, a decision now pushed into next year; Ed Miliband says they do not depend on it. Burnham says the package could add 0.5% a year to growth and is the "limit" of convergence until 2029, and defence firms got no route into the EU's future Competitiveness Fund.
