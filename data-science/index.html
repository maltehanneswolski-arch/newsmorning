AI cheating fears cost Seoul its education minister

Education minister Choi Kyo-jin offered his resignation on Sunday over the confusion caused by last-minute measures against AI-assisted cheating on the Suneung, the national university entrance exam: phones banned, checks for cameras hidden in clothing and unscheduled emergency alerts meant to expose hidden smartphones. The scare began when the top scorer in a national tax accountant exam confessed to using a miniature camera and a smartphone to get a near-perfect score, four days before the Suneung. President Lee Jae Myung has offered 100 million won ($75,000) per reported case of exam cheating and calls it a crime as serious as official corruption.

Washington's compute rule lands on Southeast Asian data centres

Commerce's interim final rule of 16 November bars data centres outside the US that run advanced American chips from serving businesses in two dozen countries including China, Iran and Russia, and shipments to Malaysia, Thailand and Singapore now need a licence. The trigger is Megaspeed, a Singapore-based operator whose former chief Alice Huang is linked to a smuggling network, and Samsung has reportedly paused some chip shipments to China under the new rules. Lawrence Wong says some US-China bifurcation may be inevitable but Singapore can still deal with both, while Bessent says remote access has been curtailed and no further restrictions are needed.

Robot agents need an embodied harness, says Grid Robotics

Grid Robotics' roadmap for agentic robotics keeps vision-language-action models (VLAs) and world action models (WAMs) as the decision core but wraps them in an embodied harness: software that supplies context and links reasoning to observations, skills, execution and verification in the physical world. The case rests on generalisation, since policy-centric models lose performance when a scene changes or an instruction is phrased differently. The test shifts from the model to the robot, which has to verify its own results and cope with a world that keeps moving while it thinks. (Turing Post)
