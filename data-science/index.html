OpenAI's agents turn up nearly a thousand Linux root exploits in one run

OpenAI says an autonomous pipeline running an unreleased cyber model validated and patched 948 local privilege-escalation flaws in the Linux kernel in a single run, at a total API cost of $1.2m, and used at least ten exploitation techniques that its own experts consider novel. Researchers typically find about 20 such Linux flaws a year; one comparison puts GPT-5.6 Sol at 166 for about $500,000 in July. OpenAI is working with Linux maintainers on disclosure and says it is putting a significant amount of frontier compute into pro bono open-source patching, at roughly $1,000 per vulnerability by Greg Brockman's count.

Voters punish data centres, and the AI bureaucrats stay in charge

Candidates who opposed data centres did especially well in the midterms, from Texas to Georgia and Ohio, while pro-AI super PACs and Elon Musk's spending fell short. Ryan Hauser argues the new Congress will check frontier AI policy rather than write it, leaving export controls, model evaluations, autonomous weapons and cyber rules with the career officials who already run them; Democrats such as Texas Representative Greg Casar still want AI regulation and investigations of tech firms near the top of the agenda. Markets read it as a threat to the build-out: Seoul's Kospi fell more than 5% on Thursday and Asian tech stocks slid.

Moonshot opens Kimi K3.1 Pro as open models chase agentic work

Moonshot AI has released the weights of Kimi K3.1 Pro, a 2.8-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 104bn active parameters and a 1m-token context, post-trained with more reinforcement learning for coding, financial research and long agentic tasks. It ranks first among open models and 13th overall on Agent Arena, with day-one support in vLLM and SGLang across AMD, Nvidia, TPU and Trainium hardware. Ricursive's Ripple-65B, a dense US model trained from scratch on 12tn tokens for a claimed $32m and licensed under Apache 2.0, claims the lead among American open models on agentic coding, and Tencent's Hy4 adds computer use for the first time.

Anthropic's watermark meets the AI Act deadline with a loophole

Anthropic is extending text watermarks to four more Claude models ahead of the EU AI Act's December deadline, but on older models such as Claude Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6, API users can disable the mark by changing a single sampling parameter, and the company's response so far is a compliance warning. India's CERT-In tells operators to stress-test their AI agents

India's computer emergency response team (CERT-In) has issued guidelines requiring checks on AI agents' permissions, task delegation and emergency-stop mechanisms, with tests for unauthorised tool use and privilege abuse; multi-agent systems must keep authenticated, logged communications and records of their actions. Its chief told MediaNama the agency now sees two kinds of agent incident: research and evaluation agents reaching live systems without authorisation, and criminals using agentic frameworks to cut the time from access to impact to a few hours.
