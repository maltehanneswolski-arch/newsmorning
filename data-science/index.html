Guterres asks for a global AI risk framework within a year

Speaking at the Bridgetown summit in Barbados on Monday, António Guterres called on member states to begin talks on a multilateral framework for managing the risks of AI and to agree on one within a year, warning that the technology is driving the biggest transfer of power in the history of humankind. Nvidia's board takes Huang back on Washington's word

Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang on Monday after input from senior levels of the US government assured it his return posed no national security risk; Trump dialled into the board meeting, and the shares rose more than 30 percent. Microsoft is first to report against OpenAI's perplexity yardstick

OpenAI has published five years of base-model log-perplexity on a fixed internal coding set: about 0.99 for GPT-3.5, 0.91 for GPT-4 and 0.76 for GPT-6 Astra, with cyber and maths abilities emerging below 0.8 and the next jump expected around 0.68 to 0.70. Microsoft AI ran MAI-2 Base on the same set and reported 0.819, the first lab to back Jakub Pachocki's call for a common limit with exit criteria such as a successor to chain-of-thought monitors. Labs train on different data, so cross-lab comparison is hard, and the proposal needs more labs, Anthropic above all, to become a real self-imposed limit.

Two groups verify a Riemann hypothesis proof found with Claude

An Anthropic researcher says he used Claude to find a proof of the Riemann hypothesis, the 167-year-old conjecture on how prime numbers are distributed; the proof passed proof-checking software, and two groups have verified it independently. It landed days after Anthropic raised $119.5 billion in the largest IPO on record, shares that fell 8.5 percent on Friday as the AI trade unravelled. Meta's chief AI officer Alexandr Wang read the result as a sign that superintelligence is near and warned that the industry is not ready.

China's robotics start-ups run out of buyers

Nearly 50 Chinese robotics makers have hit operating crises or left the market in five years, by Huxiu's count: Yinghe Robotics, once valued at $500 million, completed bankruptcy in July, and Deep Blue Robotics filed two months earlier. About 55.5 percent of the 218 embodied-AI and humanoid projects disclosed in the first half of 2026 were bought by schools and research institutes, and embodied-AI funding fell to 33.3 billion yuan in the third quarter, about 29 percent below the first-half quarterly average. One former employee says façade-cleaning robots that failed on the job were finished by human workers, with the labour folded into the project budget.
