Monetary Authority of Singapore · the central bank

Central bank and integrated financial regulator of Singapore

It runs monetary policy through the exchange rate rather than an interest rate, and supervises banks, insurers and capital markets under one roof.

Trump tells APEC that Washington opposes any globalist AI regulation

Speaking at the APEC summit in Shenzhen, Donald Trump said the US opposes any form of globalist regulation of AI and warned that robust rules on what he calls superintelligence would stifle development, even as his administration restricts access to advanced AI chips abroad. A day earlier Mark Carney called the lack of global AI governance an enormous risk, citing economic, biohazard, democratic and human-dignity dangers, and said he had set out Canada's model for that governance to Xi Jinping. The EU is pursuing a global AI pact after the Democrats won Congress.

Anthropic raises $81bn in the largest IPO on record

Anthropic priced its shares at $83 on Wednesday, above the marketed $70-78 range, and raised $81.3bn at a market value of about $1.5tn; trading on Nasdaq starts on Thursday. With a private placement and sales by its seven co-founders the total reaches $107.3bn, the first time a frontier AI lab has raised money on public markets. Venture firms that concentrated their money on the company are about to see whether that bet pays. The listing comes as Anthropic disclosed it had activated part of its responsible scaling policy, a coalition of scientists and legal scholars disputes its safety branding, and Democrats press it on governance disclosures.

OpenAI rates GPT-6.2 Astra High for AI self-improvement

OpenAI's own Preparedness assessment put GPT-6.2 Astra, released this week with the cheaper Sol, at its High threshold for AI self-improvement, a first for a model it has released publicly. Astra costs $8 per million input tokens and $40 output, Sol $2 and $10, both with a 1m-token context. On Artificial Analysis's index Astra scores 62, behind Anthropic's Fable 5.2 at 65, and Sol matches September's Astra at about 15% of the cost per task. Early testers found Astra hard to steer, one called the models essentially unusable, and Sol regressed on coding against GPT-6.1.

Senators press the AI czar after outages tied to AI-found bugs

Maria Cantwell and Mike Rounds wrote on Tuesday to Jay Clayton, the director of national intelligence and AI czar, asking him to find and fix weaknesses after outages hit the NSA's classified network, Apple, Microsoft and JPMorgan. A bipartisan bill would create a cyber and AI review board, and London regulators told City firms to tighten defences. Satya Nadella says much critical infrastructure is unpatchable.

Southeast Asia’s data-centre hubs bow to US chip approval rules

Under rules announced on Monday, data centres in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia and Brazil need US approval to buy advanced AI chips, because Washington sees them as routes for diverting compute to China, including by renting it to Chinese firms. Malaysia, which drew nearly $100bn of data-centre investment since 2021, told operators to comply. Singapore's prime minister Lawrence Wong offered to facilitate US checks on chip use and will not allow circumvention, and Indonesia is reviewing the impact. The regional build-out leaned heavily on Chinese demand.

Only nine countries back the COP31 pledge on AI’s climate footprint

The UN-backed pledge on AI's environmental impact at COP31 in Antalya is voluntary, has no binding targets and has drawn only nine countries. Alibaba is the only major technology company to sign so far; no US Big Tech firm has. The International Energy Agency forecasts that data centres will use twice as much electricity by 2030 and emit 70% more carbon, with AI three-quarters of the growth, as the US and China lean on fossil fuels. A Harvard study found 403 US hyperscale data centres emitted about 25.7m tonnes of CO2 in a year, more than Denmark.
