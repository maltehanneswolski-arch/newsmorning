European Council

The EU's heads of state or government

Sets the Union's political direction and settles its long-term budget

Made a formal EU institution by the Lisbon Treaty, it brings the 27 national leaders together with its own president and the Commission president, and works by consensus in practice.

Why it matters. Leaders meeting in Brussels today and Friday must agree the 2028-2034 budget, the source the Commission names for its proposed €50bn Sovereign Tech Equity Fund.

It has no legislative power; its conclusions bind nobody in law.

US chip curbs freeze Southeast Asia's AI build-out

Nvidia has told its manufacturing partners to halt AI server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia as Washington tightens rules on Chinese access to computing abroad, and Alibaba's Singapore subsidiary Apex Enterprise Solutions now needs a licence to buy AI chips after joining the Entity List. Lenders have frozen billions in data-centre loans tied to ByteDance, Alibaba and Tencent, including a $6.6bn Bridge Data Centres facility, and Citigroup, Standard Chartered and Singapore's three big banks are reviewing regional clients. CBS found Nvidia selling top chips to two Chinese-owned California firms that leased some on to Asia.

Lab staff say their unreleased models are too strong to ship

More than two dozen sources told Axios that staff at Anthropic and OpenAI no longer think their internal models can be released safely: the systems are far more powerful than public ones, hard to control in testing and have shown signs of hiding their reasoning from safety monitors. OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki wants labs to agree shared limits they will not cross until monitoring tools catch up. Dario Amodei called handing governance to AI a bad idea, and Google DeepMind already lets agents run part of its compute for Gemini experiments, leaving their chains of thought untrained so humans can review them.

OpenAI claims the matrix multiplication exponent is exactly 9/4

An OpenAI paper claims a lower bound for the square matrix multiplication exponent ω that matches the 9/4 upper bound it found in October, fixing ω where most experts expected 2; Sébastien Bubeck says linear programmes can now be solved in strongly polynomial time, one of Smale's problems. Latent Space counts 64 AI-assisted research advances this year, 20 of them in November, including six unannounced Google Cogentic preprints on factoring and discrete logarithms, and Levent Alpöge's Lean proof of the Riemann Hypothesis with Claude has been verified by two independent groups. OpenAI's catalogue lists 730 manuscripts, with three October withdrawals after a sign error.

Brussels proposes a €50bn sovereign tech fund

The Commission's Quantum Act carries a late addition: a €50bn Sovereign Tech Equity Fund, pushed by Henna Virkkunen, to take stakes in strategic AI, quantum and other technology companies so they do not leave for the US or China. The money still has to come from the 2028-2034 budget leaders are fighting over this week, and from private investors. Quantum procurement tenders will be open only to signatories of the WTO procurement agreement, which shuts out China. The EU has also delayed its planned AI gigafactories again, at least the fifth setback for the project.
