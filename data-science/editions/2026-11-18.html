Nvidia stops selling H200 chips in China

Acting CEO Colette Kress said on Tuesday that Nvidia will no longer sell H200 chips in China, ending its part in the deal Jensen Huang struck with Trump that let the US collect a 25% tariff on them. Third-quarter revenue rose 103% to $115.8bn, and Nvidia guided to $130bn, plus or minus 3%, for the current quarter against $125.25bn expected. The board, which has set up a committee to oversee national-security and export-control compliance, says it does not intend to bring Huang back; Trump says he would back a return.

OpenAI lets four outside labs watch its training runs

OpenAI is giving Apollo, METR, Transluce and Goodfire internal access to its models: Apollo puts six evaluators on site for four months, METR reviews OpenAI's largest reinforcement-learning run with transcripts and checkpoint snapshots, Transluce audits post-training alignment evaluations for six weeks, and Goodfire embeds interpretability researchers in training. Assessors cannot see weights or Slack, OpenAI picks which staff they interview, and METR says its scope excludes the misalignment incidents OpenAI has disclosed. OpenAI also explored buying Goodfire for more than $5bn, The Information reports.

GPT-6 Astra saturates a physics benchmark before it is published

Suvrat Raju of the International Centre for Theoretical Sciences in Bengaluru built a benchmark from 50 unpublished problems that take leading theoretical physicists about a day each. Leading models solved about 60% in September; by the time the set was finished, GPT-6 Astra answered 49 of 50 correctly from a single prompt, at under $3 of compute per answer, and found errors in the human-written solutions. Raju says there will be no second version.

OpenAI opens its Daybreak cyber model to NATO and European governments

OpenAI is giving NATO and several European governments, including Germany, France and Poland, access to Daybreak, its cybersecurity tool. NATO operators built and deployed AI agents with OpenAI's help in an exercise this month.

Singapore sets three AI missions for its financial sector

Deputy Prime Minister Gan Kim Yong set out three national AI missions for finance: preventing financial crime, widening businesses' access to credit and making AI-agent transactions safe. Korea makes OpenAI and Apple appoint a local legal representative

From 21 January, overseas platforms above set size thresholds with no address in South Korea, among them Apple, Google, Alibaba and OpenAI, must designate a domestic representative to handle consumer disputes and regulatory inquiries under the e-commerce consumer protection act. The Fair Trade Commission issued its guidelines on Wednesday and says firms that fail to comply face an immediate investigation.
