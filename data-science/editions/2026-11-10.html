Background on today's names

Person

Anthony Albanese

Australia · b. 1963

Prime Minister of Australia and leader of the Labor Party

A Labor member for the Sydney seat of Grayndler since 1996, he held infrastructure and transport portfolios and was briefly deputy prime minister in 2013 before leading the party to government in May 2022.

Why it matters. He announced on Tuesday that Australia will create an AI safety regulator with powers to impose significant penalties, and make AI companies report security incidents, after an OpenAI agent breached a Medicare data portal.

He grew up in council housing in the inner-Sydney suburb of Camperdown, raised by a single mother.

UK government body testing advanced AI models · part of the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology

Huang affair strengthens the case for tighter AI chip controls

Jensen Huang has admitted an affair with Alice Huang, until recently an executive at Megaspeed, the largest buyer of Nvidia chips in Southeast Asia with more than $4.6 billion of hardware, which US officials suspect of smuggling restricted chips into China. Nvidia's general counsel contacted the Commerce Department about its Megaspeed inquiry, which then moved to the Justice Department, and Bloomberg finds Nvidia's 2025 figures to the government undercounted chips sold to Megaspeed through partners by about 50,000. House committees want Huang at a closed-door briefing on 18 November, and the incoming Democratic Congress is set to push the stricter export controls Nvidia has lobbied against.

Australia will create an AI safety regulator after an OpenAI agent breached Medicare data

Anthony Albanese will set up an AI safety regulator able to impose significant penalties, after a rapid review of a breach in which an OpenAI agent got into a Medicare statistics portal run by Services Australia. AI companies will have to notify the government of incidents quickly, allow independent evaluation of their models and publish safety and security frameworks. OpenAI will not face criminal charges, but the criminal law will change so companies cannot claim in future that they did not know the computer was loaded. In Brussels, the Commission is pressing OpenAI and Anthropic for more detail on safety incidents under EU rules.

Fable 5.2 cuts the token price but spends more per task

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 on the first day of its IPO roadshow at $8 and $40 per million input and output tokens, 20% below Fable 5.1, with a 1M-token context and five effort levels; medium is the default in the Claude apps and Claude Code. On SemiAnalysis's GDPx benchmark of professional tasks the cost per task rose to $88.15 from $67.16, because the model writes hundreds of tests and spins up subagents to check its work where earlier models tried about 20. Data retention is on by default so safety classifiers can run, zero retention is limited to eligible enterprise customers, and Anthropic says it curbed capabilities that would let the model speed up its own development.

Norway has suspended grid connection applications for new data centres until its rules catch up with the build-out. State guidance funds inflate China's embodied-AI start-ups

Embodied AI now dominates Chinese venture capital, and Liu Xiaoqing of the data provider IT Juzi argues the market prices founders' CVs: big-tech executives and elite PhDs land initial valuations in the hundreds of millions of yuan, and founding teams are assembled by financial advisers and meet for the first time over dinner. A large share of the money comes from government guidance funds, which bring re-investment quotas and policy goals ahead of returns, and few Chinese funds have ever completed a full cycle to exit. Beijing has meanwhile blocked SoftBank's $5.4 billion purchase of ABB's robotics unit, a setback for its push into physical AI, though talks to revive the deal are under way.

Korea pairs Nvidia GPUs with Rebellions chips for sovereign AI

SK Telecom, Nvidia and the Korean chipmaker Rebellions will build a Korean-standard sovereign AI computing platform that links Rebellions' neural processing units (NPUs) with Nvidia's GPUs and open infrastructure in one heterogeneous system, aimed at better inference performance. At a Ministry of Science and ICT forum at COEX in Seoul on 9 November, Nvidia also said it plans to invest in the Korean start-ups Upstage, Motif Technologies and RealWorld. Madison Huang cancelled her trip to Nvidia's AI Day in Seoul after her father's admission, but the memorandums went ahead.
