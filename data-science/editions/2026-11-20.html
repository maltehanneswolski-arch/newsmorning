Anthropic's debut fails to stop an Asian AI sell-off

Anthropic closed its first Nasdaq day at just over $97, 17% above its $83 offer price but well off an early $111.50, after raising $81bn in an upsized listing. The Nasdaq Composite still fell more than 2%, and on Friday Asia followed: the KOSPI lost 5.21% to 4,869.96, with program trading halted for five minutes, the Nikkei dropped over 4% to its lowest since April, SoftBank fell about 16% and Taiwan's market headed for its worst week in 35 years. Nvidia's crisis and heavy spending on AI infrastructure weigh on the trade. Protesters rallied outside Nasdaq in Times Square and at Anthropic's San Francisco headquarters.

New US chip rule cuts Chinese labs off from offshore compute

Commerce Department rules closing a manufacturing loophole are biting: TSMC has halted advanced orders from Chinese chip designers and will largely limit them to older nodes, and Samsung has suspended some shipments. Moonshot AI walked away from a $150m deal for Nvidia capacity in Indonesia as overseas cloud access closes. Tencent president Martin Lau says the rule "sets everyone back in China"; Alibaba is betting on its own chips to carry its next Qwen model. Washington is also weighing a requirement for US companies to disclose their use of Chinese AI models.

Google ships Gemini 4 Neon at $3 per million input tokens

Neon is Google DeepMind's fourth Gemini 4 model in about seven weeks: $3 per million input tokens, $0.15 cached and $15 output, a 1M-token context window and four effort levels. It scored 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, matched Carbon's 60 at high effort for $3.74 per task, and placed second on the Vals Index at 70.9%, about 0.4 points behind Fable 5.2 at less than half the cost per task. It is rolling out in the paid Gemini API, Antigravity and the Gemini app, with day-one support in Cursor and GitHub Copilot; early Antigravity logs recorded 57 capacity errors in 13 sessions.

Lee orders an overhaul of Korea's state exams over AI cheating

President Lee Jae Myung on Friday called for a complete overhaul of the state exam system. A top scorer on the tax accountant exam admitted sending questions to an AI system through a hidden camera, and a man whose perfect law-school entrance score drew suspicion was found dead on Thursday; police are continuing that probe. For Thursday's Suneung, sat by more than 550,000 candidates, officials checked glasses, put stickers over clothing buttons and sent nationwide alerts to make muted phones buzz. The Education Ministry counted 111 test-takers with smart devices, five of whom used them, one with AI glasses.

A House AI safety caucus forms after GAO breaks four chatbots

The Government Accountability Office got ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok past their safeguards with common bypass techniques: two produced plans for explosive devices and two for attacks on power substations, each in under 12 hours, and GAO judged all four workable. The spring tests name no model versions, the 61-page report makes no recommendations, and the information was not new; the chatbots assembled it fast. A House AI Safety and Innovation Caucus launched with 26 founding members, seven of them Republicans despite the White House's opposition to regulating AI.

Hackers turned an open-source AI tool on the European Commission

The Commission confirmed that an AI-assisted cyberattack has hit its services since the end of October; HR and medical-reimbursement systems were taken offline, and it could not say whether staff data was stolen or whether the attack was over. CERT-EU reportedly identified ARTEX, an open-source AI hacking platform that the FBI warned last week is used by criminals and state-sponsored groups alike. Germany has put its threat level at critical as new tools make security flaws far easier to exploit.

Data centres could double their electricity use by 2030

Global electricity use by data centres could double by 2030 as AI loads grids, the IEA says, and AI data centres are set to drive a 70% rise in emissions by then as the US and China turn to fossil fuels for power. Meta is reconsidering the value-chain net-zero goal for 2030 it set in 2020, saying its plans require building the infrastructure that powers AI. Siting is turning local: Australia's Nextdc will no longer build near homes after opposition and power limits delayed an expansion, and investors are looking to Canada as US projects meet resistance.
