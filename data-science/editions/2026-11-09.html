Background on today's names

Person

Marco Rubio

United States · b. 1971

US secretary of state

The son of Cuban immigrants, born in Miami, he represented Florida in the US Senate from 2011 to 2025, where he was among the loudest China hawks, before Trump made him secretary of state in January 2025.

Why it matters. He oversaw a classified memo this spring warning the administration that Jensen Huang was in a close relationship with a suspected Chinese spy.

He went to college on a football scholarship, at Tarkio College in Missouri.

Nvidia's board investigates whether its chief was a spy target

Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation on 8 November after Jensen Huang admitted an affair with Alice Huang, an executive at Singapore's Megaspeed whom US officials suspect of working for Chinese intelligence; Megaspeed bought at least $4.8bn of Nvidia chips. Marco Rubio oversaw a classified memo this spring warning about the relationship, and in an interview filmed on 14 October Huang called China not our enemy and US chip policy a mistake. Trump says he should stay on. Korean chipmakers fear tighter export compliance if Washington steps up scrutiny of AI chip shipments to China, and Madison Huang cancelled a Seoul visit on the eve of her arrival.

The White House pulls the US AI safety centre closer

Officials at CAISSI, the Commerce Department's small AI safety centre, have been asked to report informally to a White House office, the Associated Press reports. That puts the unit's independence in doubt just as public anxiety grows over reports of AI agents running out of control. Trump told CBS after the midterm defeat that he does not mind guardrails but will not put the US in a deep second position; China, he said, has none. Analysts quoted by the FT say the Democratic win gives China a window to catch up if the new Congress takes a tougher line on data-centre construction.

Rebellions chips get a way into Nvidia's data-centre racks

Nvidia, SK Telecom and Rebellions signed a memorandum at Nvidia's AI Day Seoul on Monday to link Rebellions' neural processing units (NPUs) with Nvidia GPUs through NVLink Fusion, which lets third-party chips plug into Nvidia's systems, as SK Telecom builds AI data-centre infrastructure. The deal sits inside a wider pact with Korea's science ministry covering chips, data centres, talent, start-ups, models, agents and security. Seoul says the spy allegations will not touch supply: 10,000 GPUs funded under the 2025 supplementary budget have arrived and are in use.
