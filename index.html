Washington's chip licence rule lands on Singapore and Malaysia

A Commerce Department interim rule issued on Monday requires a licence to export, re-export or transfer advanced AI chips to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil, to stop Chinese firms renting US compute in overseas data centres. The Bureau of Industry and Security said the Treasury and the National Security Council wrote it, which experts read as a rift with Howard Lutnick's department, and Commerce is running a criminal investigation into Singapore's Megaspeed. Singapore wants trusted access; Trump says he would not mind Jensen Huang returning to Nvidia, whose interim chief Colette Kress reports tonight after the Nasdaq fell 2.5%.
