Europe rejects Kushner's plan to redraw its security with Moscow

Ahead of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi on 10-11 November, Jared Kushner has proposed a new security architecture for Europe, and Trump hails a 'big, beautiful agreement' for 'all of Europe' after meeting Putin's envoy Kirill Dmitriev. Capitals fear a deal that sidelines allies and restores a Russian sphere of influence at NATO's expense; Kaja Kallas says Putin has no veto over European security, and Estonia's Kristen Michal wants sanctions to suffocate Moscow rather than attempts to 'please the dictator'. Moscow's team under Vladimir Medinsky is back to demanding a smaller Ukrainian army and recognition of Crimea and Donbas. German defence ministry notes warn that Washington is watching Europe's push for arms independence.

Wall Street's rebound on Friday was feeble by historical standards.

The Commission halves its legislative plans for 2027

The Commission's eight-page work programme for 2027 carries half as many initiatives as the one it tabled in 2025, after Giorgia Meloni, Friedrich Merz and Bart De Wever demanded less Brussels legislation and revived the idea of an emergency brake on EU measures. It adds a deterrence plan aimed at China and winds back parts of the Green Deal, with a review of the Birds and Habitats Directives under consideration for next autumn. The draft warns that the 2030 defence readiness target is at risk despite unprecedented funding. Separately, proposals tied to EU money, AI chips and the Arctic among them, are stuck while capitals fight over the size of the 2028-2034 budget.
