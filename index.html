Person

Marco Rubio

United States · b. 1971

US Secretary of State

Born in Miami to Cuban immigrant parents, he represented Florida in the Senate from 2011 and became Secretary of State in January 2025, after the Senate confirmed him without a dissenting vote.

Why it matters. He called the first Abu Dhabi round very productive, wants Europe at the table, and said on Tuesday that major combat operations against Iran are no longer needed.

He is the first Hispanic American to serve as US Secretary of State.

NATO

North Atlantic Treaty Organization · Brussels

Transatlantic defence alliance of 32 members · the North Atlantic Council decides by consensus

Founded by the Washington Treaty of April 1949; under Article 5 an armed attack on one member is treated as an attack on all. Finland joined in 2023 and Sweden in 2024.

Why it matters. The Europeans and Americans signed a joint line on NATO before the Abu Dhabi talks opened, as Washington floats a new security framework for Ukraine, Russia and Europe.

Washington floats a new European security framework in Abu Dhabi

Russian, Ukrainian and US officials held nearly four hours of talks in Abu Dhabi on Tuesday, the first trilateral round since February, and meet again on Wednesday. Washington proposed a new security framework covering Ukraine, Russia and Europe; Volodymyr Zelensky called the day constructive, and the Russian delegation, which resists a ceasefire, said nothing. Before the talks the EU, France, Germany, Italy, the UK, Ukraine, the US and NATO signed a statement keeping NATO central to Euro-Atlantic defence. Kaja Kallas says Moscow is only pretending to negotiate, and Zelensky wants EU leaders to pick their envoy for the talks quickly and consult Kyiv on the choice.

Ships return to Hormuz without the oil

Nearly 770 vessels crossed the Strait of Hormuz in October, close to July's wartime peak of 795, but transponder data put the oil they carried out at 39.5 million barrels, about a quarter of July's 151.3 million. Iran is attacking tankers on the southern route near Oman, and an Iranian lawmaker says ships from India, China, Iraq and Qatar are exempt; Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Iraq and Kuwait still exported 15.5 million barrels a day in October via Yanbu and the Fujairah pipeline. Marco Rubio said on Tuesday that major combat operations are no longer needed because economic pressure is hurting Tehran enough, and Iran says its offer to reopen the strait within two days on its own terms still stands.

Commerce keeps Megaspeed off the entity list

The Commerce Department has delayed listing Megaspeed, the Singapore cloud company tied to Alice Huang that is suspected of smuggling Nvidia chips into China and is still buying them. The gap dates from May 2025, when the administration stopped enforcing the Biden-era licence requirement for big AI chip orders to untrusted firms in third countries and never wrote a replacement; $80 billion in AI chips went to Singapore, Malaysia and Thailand this year, and $4.8 billion shipped while Commerce held the intelligence, says Chris McGuire of the Council on Foreign Relations. Reuters found that Northwestern Polytechnical University, which has close PLA ties and is on the US blacklist, bought H200 chips last month.

Gibran offers to quit after the wiretap arrests

Indonesia's vice-president, Gibran Rakabuming Raka, says he intends to resign; the palace denies that Prabowo Subianto refused the request but has not said he accepted it. The Supreme Court ruled in October that Gibran had not proved he finished high school, a legal requirement for the post, and parliament was moving toward impeachment. Police last week arrested former police chief Listyo Sigit Prabowo and two other generals on suspicion of tapping the president, one of some 6,000 people allegedly surveilled since 2025, and the palace calls it treason. Listyo is seen as Joko Widodo's man, and the exit of Widodo's son could end the alliance between the two camps.

Merz needs billions for his electricity tax cut before the budget closes

Coalition budget leaders again failed on Tuesday night to agree the electricity tax relief Friedrich Merz promised at the CSU congress, and the finance ministry plans an updated adjustment draft before Lars Klingbeil faces the Bundestag budget committee on Wednesday afternoon. Karin Prien's parental allowance reform goes to cabinet with couples keeping 14 months rather than the planned 12.

Deputy ambassadors try to define Made in Europe

EU deputy ambassadors meet on Wednesday to settle which countries count as European and what counts as low carbon under the industrial law's Made in Europe rules, as the Irish presidency pushes for a member-state deal before the end of the year. London wants in: a deal on emissions trading and the carbon border tax is close before the EU-UK summit on 20 November, and Britain is courting access to Made in Europe preferences. Hamish Falconer, the minister running the EU talks, says the UK has not accepted linking electricity trade to the cohesion fund but will discuss paying toward infrastructure in poorer member states in return for access to the EU electricity market.
