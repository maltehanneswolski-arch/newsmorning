Xi's purge reaches Shanghai's party chief

Xinhua said on Tuesday that Chen Jining, Shanghai's party secretary and a Politburo member, is under investigation by the CCDI (Central Commission for Discipline Inspection) for suspected serious violations of discipline and law, with no reason given. He is the fourth member of the 20th Politburo to fall, after He Weidong, Ma Xingrui and Zhang Youxia; four members of a serving Politburo were last investigated in October 1976, when the Gang of Four were detained, and rumour had pointed at Chongqing's Yuan Jiajun instead. No Shanghai party chief had been investigated since Chen Liangyu in 2006, and Chen, 62, had steered the AI industry in both Beijing and Shanghai.

Hybrid attacks push the EU towards fewer Russian visas

EU ambassadors take up Russia visa policy on Wednesday, with a Council note urging capitals to back a common line on non-essential travel that would "considerably reduce" Russian tourism to the Schengen area. Washington readies overcapacity tariffs on the EU

The Trump administration is preparing Section 301 tariffs on 16 trading partners, among them the EU, China, Japan, South Korea and Taiwan, after a probe into excess industrial capacity; the duties would vary by partner and stack on existing levies, with steel, aluminium and cars possibly exempt. Trade representative Jamieson Greer is to present them as a rebuild of the global tariffs the Supreme Court struck down in February. Most EU goods pay 10% today, Ifo puts this year's tariff damage to German growth at 0.6 points, and the Democratic Congress cannot override a veto, so the courts are Europe's better lever.

North Korea declared Sylvain Astier, head of Switzerland's new diplomatic office, persona non grata on Wednesday and ordered the office shut, calling Bern's sanctions on the Songdowon children's camp an "open hostile act".
