Person 🇨🇳

Xi Jinping

b. 1953 · China

General Secretary of the Communist Party of China, President of the People's Republic, Chairman of the Central Military Commission

Holds the three posts that matter at once, which no predecessor since Deng has combined for this long. His consolidation runs through the word core, a designation the party awarded him in 2016 and which the Long March speech now projects backwards onto Mao as the condition for victory.

Why it matters. The fifth plenum of the 20th Central Committee meets within days to frame the agenda into next year's Party Congress, and Thursday's anniversary speech is the curtain-raiser for it. Reading what he added and dropped against the 2016 version is the cheapest available indicator of what the plenum will prioritise.

He spent seven years in a cave dwelling in Liangjiahe, Shaanxi, after being sent down during the Cultural Revolution, and has returned to the village repeatedly since.

People's Bank of China

中国人民银行 · the central bank

Monetary policy, the exchange rate and capital-account opening

Operates under the State Council rather than independently of it, so its rate and currency decisions are political as well as technical. It also runs the payment infrastructure that renminbi internationalisation depends on.

Why it matters. A fifth plenum sets the five-year economic frame, and the bank is the institution that has to deliver it while retail investors are taking losses on A-shares. Whatever growth language comes out of the plenum, the currency and credit settings are where it becomes observable.

It ran the world's first large-scale central bank digital currency trials, years before any G7 peer left the study stage.

Costa puts a date on the budget and on who pays for Ukraine

Agreement on the next multiannual financial framework is possible in November, European Council president Antonio Costa told Rapporteur, and leaders could settle Ukraine's financing as soon as 26 November. The Commission wants the EU to carry two thirds of Kyiv's fresh 70 billion euro shortfall and a political agreement before the end of the year rather than a negotiation that runs into 2027. Parliament is pulling at the same money from the other side: MEPs added 7 billion euros to Erasmus+, trimmed the digital and space envelopes, asked for 136.1 billion euros for defence and space, and set agriculture against transport and housing inside the new European Competitiveness Fund. None of that has been conceded by the frugal capitals, so a November deal means leaders fixing a headline number while the line-by-line fight is still open. The two numbers worth carrying forward are the two-thirds share and the 26 November date, because they define what Kyiv can plan against and what the member states will be asked to underwrite.

Beijing invites Brussels into its AI club while both argue over who conceded

China has offered the European Commission a seat in its AI alliance, an invitation that arrives while the EU executive already sits inside the rival American initiative, so accepting would be a choice rather than an addition. The structural point underneath all of it is that up to 90 percent of global antibiotic active ingredients are now made outside Europe, mostly in China, which is leverage that no trade truce reverses.

Crude at 114 dollars meets an AI trade that has started to wobble

Oil reached 114 dollars for the first time since May, and the mechanism is diesel rather than crude supply. Gulf crude exports last week ran above pre-war levels, yet the diesel premium over crude is above 84 dollars, higher than during the 2022 energy crisis, so refineries buy every available barrel and process it immediately, which pulls the crude price up behind the product. Freight costs have risen sevenfold on Goldman Sachs figures. Trump's loosening of diesel sanctions on Russia changes little at that scale: the first deliveries amount to about 210,000 tonnes, roughly one day of US diesel exports. On the other side of the ledger the AI trade is being marked down, with OpenAI trimming its valuation target to 1.25 trillion dollars, Calpers cutting its Magnificent 7 holdings, and the Kospi opening 2.36 percent lower before falling 2.83 percent to 5,377.17, which is energy costs and technology valuations hitting European industrial planning from both directions at once.

A decade-old flaw in the web's plumbing, found by AI and leaked on Tuesday

Vulnerabilities in PHP sat undetected for more than a decade, were first found by AI and then leaked on Tuesday, and the estimate is that some 30 million websites are exposed. Governments in Europe and elsewhere pulled websites offline as a precaution on Wednesday, with banking, airline, rail, tax and public services among the emergency shutdowns, and Cloudflare logged millions of attack attempts once the flaws were public. The detail that should change procurement thinking is how the attack was built: the individual flaws were known and rated non-critical, and AI chained them into a usable path, which Marc Henrichmann, who chairs the Bundestag's intelligence oversight panel, called a turning point. Satya Nadella said developers had one of the longest and hardest nights in a long time, and Handelsblatt's reading is that updates and security checks alone no longer suffice when attackers get cheaper and faster. For a defence-industrial base being asked to scale quickly, the cost side of that asymmetry is the part nobody has budgeted.
