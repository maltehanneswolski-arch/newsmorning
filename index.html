Know the players

Background on today's names

Person

Hakeem Jeffries

United States · b. 1970

Leader of the House Democrats since January 2023 · representative for New York's 8th district

A Brooklyn lawyer who sat in the New York State Assembly before entering Congress in 2013, he succeeded Nancy Pelosi as the party's leader in the House and has run the caucus from the minority for the whole of Trump's second term so far.

Why it matters. Democrats took the House in Tuesday's midterms, which puts him in line for the speakership and gives him the committee chairs, subpoenas and appropriations bills that decide how hard Congress scrutinises the Iran war and tariff policy.

If the new House elects him in January he becomes the first Black speaker, second in the line of presidential succession after the vice president.

Institution

Democratic Party

Democratic Party · United States

One of the two major US parties · majority in the Senate and the House from January 2027

Founded in 1828 around Andrew Jackson's campaign, it now controls the chamber that writes appropriations and the one that confirms nominees and ratifies treaties, while the executive keeps the delegated tariff and sanctions powers.

Why it matters. It flipped Republican Senate seats in North Carolina and Texas and won the House, ending unified Republican control two years before Trump leaves office.

It is generally counted as the oldest active political party in the world.

The Commission's 2027 plan swaps new laws for implementation and deterrence

The Commission's eight-page draft 2027 work programme, due today, moves the executive from new legislation to implementing existing law and easing the burden on national governments [Rapporteur PM]. It floats an anti-China 'deterrence pact' for Europe, warns that the 2030 defence readiness target is at risk, abandons the energy taxation overhaul and the anti-greenwashing bill, and keeps a 'legislative or non-legislative' review of core nature laws for next autumn, which Berlin, Madrid and Paris have already warned against [EURACTIV]. The one sizeable new file is a digital transformation package to raise AI use.
